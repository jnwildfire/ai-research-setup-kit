# Dictation dictionary

The names and terms that dictation and transcription get wrong, in one table. This note is the
source; the files the export script writes are generated from it and never edited by hand. Copy
this note into your vault, delete the example rows you do not need, and add your own.

- **Term**: the word as it should be written.
- **Category**: one of person, organisation, place, product, project, abbreviation, method,
  software, other-language (a word of another language that dictation turns into English).
- **Heard as**: the garbles you have actually seen, separated by `;`. Record only what you saw.
- **Replace**: the garbles that are never a real word in your texts, so a blind replacement is
  safe. "Zatero" is never meant, so it is listed. "cloud" is often meant, so "cloud" for Claude is
  heard but not replaced. When in doubt, leave it out.
- **Note**: anything a later reader needs, such as why a garble is not replaced.

A name of a person goes in only if that person would not mind: the dictionary is sent to the
dictation service when you import it.
